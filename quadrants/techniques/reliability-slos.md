name: Reliability: SLOs & error budgets
ring: adopt
---
Pick the few journeys that matter, set service-level objectives, and treat the error budget as the release gate: when it is spent, reliability work beats new features. Tie it to on-call, incident response and blameless postmortems, and rehearse restores against explicit RTO and RPO targets instead of assuming backups work.

The cost is measurement and discipline. Adopt once anything is user-facing; skip only for genuinely throwaway systems.
