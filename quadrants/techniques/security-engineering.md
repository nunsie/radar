Shift security left as engineering rather than a gate: threat modelling on new designs, least privilege with short-lived credentials and OIDC instead of long-lived keys, secrets in a manager rather than env files, and a supply chain you can attest to — dependency review, SBOM, signed artifacts.

Adopt everywhere. The cost is ongoing hygiene; the cost of skipping it is discovered on the worst possible day.
