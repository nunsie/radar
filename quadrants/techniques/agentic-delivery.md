---
name: Agentic delivery
ring: trial
---
Agents as a real part of the delivery loop: tools, context and multi-step goals rather than fancy autocomplete, and a pipeline where a well-specified change goes in and a reviewed change comes out, gated by strong automated checks. Humans keep the design, the interfaces and the final call.

Trial: promising on well-specified, well-tested, low-ambiguity work; not for requirements still being discovered or failures that are expensive. The checks are what make it safe, so invest there first.
