Shipping AI as a dependency, not just using it to write code: evaluations as tests, guardrails against prompt injection and unsafe output, and a deliberate choice between prompting, retrieval and fine-tuning. Route and fall back across models, watch cost and latency per request, and treat context and retrieval like any other data pipeline — including privacy and leakage.

Trial: the capability is real, the operating practices are still forming. Keep a human on the failure path for anything consequential.
