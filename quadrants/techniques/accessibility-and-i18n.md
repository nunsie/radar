name: Accessibility & i18n
ring: assess
---
Accessibility and internationalisation as architectural constraints, not a later pass: semantic structure and keyboard paths, text externalised from the start, locale-aware dates, currency and layout, and tests that cover both. Retrofitting either is a rewrite of the view layer.

Assess where the audience or the regulation demands it; the cost is discipline up front, and the cost of delay is much larger.
