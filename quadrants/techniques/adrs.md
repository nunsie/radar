name: ADRs / design docs
ring: adopt
---
Write down the why, not just the what — future me is the main audience. Lightweight ADRs beat tribal memory, and a regular design-review cadence is what turns them from a graveyard into a decision log: bring the interfaces and the trade-offs, not the finished code.

Adopt as a habit, not a tool. The cost is a page per real decision and a review people actually attend; skip it for reversible, local changes and spend the time on the one-way doors.
