name: Delivery metrics
ring: trial
---
Measure the pipeline, not the people: lead time for change, deployment frequency, change failure rate and time to restore, plus the developer-experience signals that explain them. Numbers make "we need to slow down" arguable with evidence rather than opinion.

Trial: cheap to start and genuinely useful, but easy to weaponise. Keep it about the system, agree the definitions, and do not trust a trend before you trust the data.
