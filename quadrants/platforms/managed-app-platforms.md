ring: assess
---
Managed app hosting — Vercel, Netlify, Railway and similar — for prototypes, side projects and small frontends where the developer experience is excellent and shipping fast is the point. The architectural question is not which vendor ranks best but exit cost: pricing cliffs, egress, and how much of the app is portable to another host.

Assess for anything with real traffic or a long life; adopt only when lock-in is acceptable because the app is small or genuinely disposable.
